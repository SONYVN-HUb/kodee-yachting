Sailing catamaran or motor yacht? How to pick the right boat for your group

9 min read · Updated 20/11/2026

Every boat has a personality. Some are born to dash out to the islands; others are made to sit still in a bay while you sunbathe on the bow nets. Pick the wrong personality and the trip is still fun — just not the trip you imagined. This post helps you choose right, based on the specs Theia Yachting publishes for its ten yachts.

Question 1: How many people are in your group?

This is the strongest filter, because the price is per boat, not per person. A big boat with few people is a waste; a small boat with many people is a squeeze.

  • 2–5 people: Merry Fisher 795 – Jade (up to 5 guests, Nha Trang) is the most compact choice; Princess V40 – Pearl (up to 6 guests) if you want a sportier motor yacht.
  • 6–15 people: Princess 56 – Diamond and 1460 – Blue (up to 12 guests), 1160 Lite – Silver and Lagoon 450F – Azure (up to 15 guests).
  • 16–29 people: Coral and Coral II (up to 28 guests), Titan speed yacht (up to 29 guests).
  • 30+ people: 1580 – Onyx in Phu Quoc, up to 42 guests.

Question 2: Are you in Phu Quoc or Nha Trang?

Theia operates in two areas. In Nha Trang, boats depart from Ana Marina; the fleet there includes Coral, Blue, Silver, Pearl, Diamond, Jade and Titan. In Phu Quoc there are currently three boats: Coral II, Onyx and Lagoon 450F – Azure. If you're staying in Phu Quoc the list is shorter, but all three are sailing catamarans — the best fit for a slow day around the small islands.

Question 3: What kind of feeling do you want?

If you want space and smoothness — choose a catamaran. Catamarans have two hulls, so the deck is much wider relative to the length. Coral II is 13.6 m long but 6.9 m wide; Lagoon 450F – Azure is 13.96 m long and a full 7.87 m wide. The result: lots of outdoor space, lounging nets at the bow and a “home on the water” feeling. Theia describes these boats as having a stable twin-hull design — something anyone prone to seasickness will appreciate.

If you want speed and style — choose a motor yacht. Princess V40 – Pearl tops out at 34–36 knots according to Theia's specs. Princess 56 – Diamond is 18.11 m long, with a flybridge and both indoor and outdoor space. And Titan — a 20.8 m speed yacht with twin 700 hp Hyundai engines — is offered for fast island runs and custom itineraries, with an air-conditioned salon.

If you want a design story — Lagoon 450F – Azure was built by Lagoon (France), with naval architecture by VPLP Design, exterior by Patrick le Quément and interior by Nauta Design (Italy). Azure is also the only boat on the list that explicitly includes a private chef on board, with lunch or dinner cooked fresh on deck.

Choose the boat for the moment you want to remember, not the photo you want to post.

Question 4: What's your budget?

Listed prices for a half-day (5-hour) whole-boat charter, from Theia's website on 20/11/2026:

  • Merry Fisher 795 – Jade: $590 · up to 5 guests · Nha Trang
  • Titan speed yacht: $1350 · up to 29 guests · Nha Trang
  • Catamaran 1360 – Coral: $1390 · up to 28 guests · Nha Trang
  • 1460 – Blue: $1490 · up to 12 guests · Nha Trang
  • Princess V40 – Pearl: $1490 · up to 6 guests · Nha Trang
  • 1360 – Coral II: $1490 · up to 28 guests · Phu Quoc
  • 1160 Lite – Silver: $1590 · up to 15 guests · Nha Trang
  • 1580 – Onyx: $1590 · up to 42 guests · Phu Quoc
  • Lagoon 450F – Azure: $2690 · up to 15 guests · Phu Quoc
  • Princess 56 – Diamond: $2990 · up to 12 guests · Nha Trang

A simple way to compare fairly: divide the price by the number of people actually going. A group of 28 on Coral II pays a very different amount per person than 6 people on the same boat. Full-day (10-hour) trips and VND quotes: message us for a quote.

Three scenarios, three picks

A couple planning a proposal in Phu Quoc: Lagoon 450F – Azure for privacy and an onboard chef, or Coral II if you want fireworks on the evening tour (schedule to be confirmed with us).

A 40-person company in Phu Quoc: 1580 – Onyx — up to 42 guests, sound system, BBQ area, free Wi-Fi, freshwater shower and toilets on board.

A family of 4 in Nha Trang on a tight budget: Merry Fisher 795 – Jade, with SUP boards, masks and snorkels and fishing gear already in the package.

Sources: Theia Yachting's yacht pages and FAQ (theiayachting.com/vi), accessed 20/11/2026. Prices and specs may change; please contact us to confirm before booking.

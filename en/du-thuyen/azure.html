Catamaran Lagoon 450F – Azure

📍 Phu Quoc👥 Up to 15 guests⏱ Half day · 5 hours⚓ Built in 2019💎 $2690 / whole boat
Overview

Lagoon 450 F – Azure is a spacious, elegant sailing catamaran made for private cruises around Phu Quoc. Modern design, airy spaces and the signature stability of a twin-hull yacht make it a relaxed, refined way to enjoy the waters of southern Vietnam.

At 13.96 m long and 7.87 m wide, the Lagoon 450 F offers noticeably more space than many catamarans in its class. The wide hull creates plenty of comfortable outdoor areas, while the salon and cockpit are laid out for dining, chatting and lounging throughout the trip. Azure's highlight is its generous flybridge, where guests take in panoramic views of the sea and the small offshore islands.

The Lagoon 450 F was developed by French shipyard Lagoon together with naval architects VPLP Design, exterior designer Patrick le Quément and Italian interior studio Nauta Design. The result is a catamaran that sails steadily and offers a bright, airy living space with a distinctly European design spirit.

With a capacity of up to 15 day guests, Azure is ideal for private trips with family and friends, celebrations or simply an easy day at sea. Guests can move freely between the cockpit, the open deck and the salon, choosing the spot that suits them for sun, sea breeze or a relaxed meal.

On a private Lagoon 450 F – Azure charter, guests can swim, snorkel, fish and paddle a SUP in the waters around Phu Quoc. Meals are prepared on board by the chef, with drinks and beer included, so you can enjoy every minute at sea without worrying about anything else.

Whether you go for a 5-hour private cruise or a full day at sea, Lagoon 450 F – Azure gives you a private, spacious and refined space to see Phu Quoc from a whole new angle.

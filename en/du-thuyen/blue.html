Sailing Catamaran 1460 – Blue

📍 Nha Trang👥 Up to 12 guests⏱ Half day · 5 hours⚓ Built in 2021💎 $1490 / whole boat
Overview

Luxury Sailing Catamaran 1460 – Blue offers a premium private charter in Nha Trang, combining stylish design, modern technology and the comfort of a high-end catamaran.

This 14.6-metre hybrid sailing catamaran runs on an advanced hybrid propulsion system — smooth, quiet and kinder to the environment. It's ideal for anyone who loves privacy, comfort and a different kind of holiday: families, birthday parties, company events, romantic proposals or sunset cruises.

The wide twin-hull design gives plenty of outdoor space to relax. Lounge on the bow nets, soak up the views of Nha Trang Bay or dine in the spacious outdoor area at the stern.

Inside, the large air-conditioned salon has panoramic windows that let in natural light and stunning ocean views. The U-shaped sofa is the perfect spot for meals, chats and chilling throughout the trip.

Every charter is carefully prepared so you can fully enjoy your holiday. Swim, snorkel, SUP, fish or just relax on deck while the professional crew takes care of every detail.

Half day or full day, Luxury Sailing Catamaran 1460 – Blue always delivers the perfect mix of comfort, personalised service and unforgettable moments at sea.

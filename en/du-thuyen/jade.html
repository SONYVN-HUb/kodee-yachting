Motor Yacht Merry Fisher 795 – Jade

📍 Nha Trang👥 Up to 5 guests⏱ Half day · 5 hours💎 $590 / whole boat
Overview

Motor Yacht Merry Fisher 795 – Jade is a great fit if you love small, private trips and want to enjoy a day at sea in the most relaxed way.

With room for up to 5 guests, this yacht offers a private space for families, couples or small groups of friends. No big-group schedule — take your time watching the sea, enjoy the cool breeze and spend quality time with your people.

At the bow there's a spacious sunbathing area, perfect for relaxing and enjoying the view. The rear outdoor seating is a comfy spot to rest while cruising or at anchor in a quiet bay. Inside, a comfortable cabin has everything you need for a day at sea.

Throughout the trip you can swim, snorkel, fish or paddle a SUP. Drinks, fresh fruit and light snacks are served on board, so you can enjoy the trip without worrying about the little details.

Merry Fisher 795 – Jade is perfect for family trips, small groups of friends or a private relaxing day at sea, when you want to enjoy Nha Trang at a gentler, easier pace.

Programme

PRIVATE CHARTER | 5 HOURS

Five hours at sea is ideal if you want to escape everyday life for a while and see Nha Trang from a different angle.

Stop to swim and snorkel, try fishing, paddle a SUP or just relax on the bow sun pad and take in the view. Drinks, fresh fruit and light snacks are served on board.

Compact and private, this yacht is perfect for couples, families or small groups who love a private, relaxed trip.

Speed Yacht – TITAN

📍 Nha Trang👥 Up to 29 guests⏱ Half day · 5 hours⚓ Built in 2024💎 $1350 / whole boat
Overview

Titan is a roomy speed yacht designed for comfortable sea days and fast island hopping in Vietnam. With room for up to 29 guests, it's ideal for big groups who want to move fast on the water and still have plenty of space to relax, eat and enjoy the ride.

At 20.8 m long, Titan has generous indoor and outdoor space. Use the air-conditioned salon or relax on the open deck. The shaded area keeps you out of the sun throughout the trip, while the big shared dining table makes it easy to eat and hang out together.

On board there's an air-conditioned salon, a toilet, a freshwater shower and a sound system. During the trip you can swim, snorkel or paddle a SUP, with all the gear ready on board.

Lunch is freshly prepared by the chef, with beer and soft drinks. Towels, snorkeling gear, SUP boards and life jackets are also provided, so you can enjoy your time at sea without packing much.

Combining speed, capacity and comfort, Titan suits big groups, island-hopping routes and custom sea trips. If you need to get somewhere fast by sea or want a day out with a large group, the Theia Yachting team will recommend the right itinerary and yacht.

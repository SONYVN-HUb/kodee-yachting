Sailing Catamaran 1160 Lite – Silver

📍 Nha Trang👥 Up to 15 guests⏱ Half day · 5 hours⚓ Built in 2019💎 $1590 / whole boat
Overview

Sailing Catamaran 1160 Lite – Silver is the perfect pick for a relaxing day at sea in a private, upscale setting. With its modern design, smart layout and open, airy space, Silver delivers a memorable cruise amid the stunning scenery of Nha Trang Bay.

Designed for families, couples and small groups of friends, this 11.6-metre catamaran strikes the perfect balance between comfort, stability and closeness to nature. It's the ideal space for birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, get-togethers or simply a full day off at sea.

The indoor living space flows seamlessly into the outdoor areas, creating a spacious, comfortable feel. The aft dining area is ideal for lunch or a glass of champagne with an ocean view, while the bow nets are always a favourite spot for sunbathing, sea-gazing and capturing great moments.

Throughout the trip you can swim, snorkel, paddle a SUP, fish or simply relax on deck and enjoy the fresh Nha Trang sea air.

Whether you choose 5 or 10 hours, Silver 1160 always delivers attentive service, privacy and memorable moments with family and friends.

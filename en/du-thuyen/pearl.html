Princess V40 Yacht – Pearl

📍 Nha Trang👥 Up to 6 guests⏱ Half day · 5 hours💎 $1490 / whole boat
Overview

Luxury Motor Yacht V40 – Pearl is ideal if you want a day at sea in a private, comfortable and refined space. With its elegant, sporty lines and impressive performance, this yacht is perfect for couples, families and small groups who want to explore Nha Trang their own way.

The yacht takes up to 6 guests, giving you a private, comfortable space without sharing the trip with other groups. The front sun pad is perfect for relaxing, catching the breeze and admiring Nha Trang Bay, while the interior is a comfy retreat when you need a break from the sun.

Throughout the trip you can swim, snorkel, fish or simply relax and enjoy the beautiful seascape. The yacht has everything you need for a day at sea, including a freshwater shower, towels, Wi-Fi and a fully equipped galley.

Food and drink menus can be tailored to your taste and served on board. Perfect for a family trip, a romantic date, a special anniversary or simply a relaxing day between the Nha Trang sea and sky.

Pearl combines the flexibility of a motor yacht with a private, comfortable space, so you can enjoy Nha Trang at your own rhythm.

Programme

PRIVATE CHARTER | 5 HOURS

Enjoy a few relaxing hours aboard the Princess V40 and discover Nha Trang Bay in a private space — no shared bookings.

Swim, snorkel, fish or relax on the front sun pad while taking in the sea and the Nha Trang coastline. Meals and drinks can be prepared on board to your group's taste.

Compact and private, the Princess V40 is perfect for couples, families and small groups of friends.

PRIVATE CHARTER | 10 HOURS

Spend a whole day at sea and enjoy Nha Trang Bay your own way, no rush.

Swim, snorkel, fish, relax on deck and enjoy food and drinks on board. The schedule can be adjusted to your group's wishes and the weather on the day.
